MarketLab — ER diagram: notation and contents

Notation of the course slides (Ramakrishnan & Gehrke, chapter 2). Every symbol below is drawn exactly as on the diagram pages; the examples are relationship sets of MarketLab.

Basic symbols
Entity set (rectangle): a collection of similar objects, stored as one table.
Attribute (oval) of the entity set it is attached to. The key attribute, which identifies each entity, is underlined.
Derived attribute (dashed oval): computed from other data. An order's total is the sum over its lines.
Relationship set (diamond): connects the entity sets on its lines. An oval on the diamond is a descriptive attribute of the relationship (here: since when the staff member works for the seller).
Role label on a line: when one entity set takes part twice in a relationship, each line names its role.
Cardinality and participation (read the lines at the diamond)
Thin line, no arrow: no constraint. A seller may employ many staff members and a staff member may work for many sellers: many-to-many (M:N).
Arrow into the diamond = key constraint (at most one). Each ticket is about at most one order; an order may have many tickets: one-to-many (1:N). The arrow is on the line of the entity set that is in the relationship at most once.
Thick line = total participation (at least one). Every courier serves at least one delivery zone. A thin line is partial participation: zero is allowed.
Thick line with arrow = exactly one. Every order is placed by exactly one customer; a customer places any number of orders (1:N).
Arrows on both lines: one-to-one (1:1). Each payment is for exactly one order, and an order has at most one payment.
Advanced constructs
Weak entity set (thick rectangle) and its identifying relationship (thick diamond). The weak entity has no key of its own: its partial key (dashed underline) is unique only within its owner, so an order line is identified by order_id + line_no. The thick arrow says each line belongs to exactly one order.
Ternary relationship: one diamond joins three entity sets. A stock position is one (seller, variant, warehouse) combination with its available quantity.
Aggregation (dashed box): the Stocks relationship together with its entity sets is treated as one entity set, so another relationship can connect to it. A line that ends at the box (Reserves) relates to a whole stock position.
ISA hierarchy (triangle): the subclasses inherit the key and attributes of Accounts. overlap: no = an account is in at most one subclass; covering: yes = every account is in one of them.
How the pages are drawn
Area pages: a grey dashed rectangle is an entity set of another area, named with its area in brackets. Its attributes are on its own area page.
Overview page: a grey rounded box groups the entity sets of one business area. It only helps reading; it is not an ER construct. Relationships between two areas are drawn outside the boxes.
